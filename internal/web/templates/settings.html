Intervals take a number and a unit, such as 30m, 6h or 1d; off stops that kind of scheduled poll. Quick polls run every 5m or slower, full polls every 1h or slower, and quick must be more frequent than full. A quick poll lists programs and fetches scope for new ones (a few requests). A full poll refreshes every program's scope (about one request per program). A disabled platform isn't polled or logged in to; its stored programs stay browsable. Sign-in says whether the platform accepted each credential the last time it was used; a rejected one says why (fix it in {{if .CanSave}}Accounts{{else}}.env and restart{{end}}). A browser sync reads what only the logged-in website shows (Intigriti's API leaves it out), every 6h or slower, once a day by default; it reuses its saved session and opens Chrome only when the platform asks for the password again, and if that login fails it tries again after 1h, then 2h, 4h… While it's on, full polls leave the program stats to it.
